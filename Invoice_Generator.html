Status follows approval: Open = nothing approved yet (draft / in-review invoices only show in their own column); In progress = approved or partly shipped; Fulfilled = fully shipped; Over-shipped = approved + shipped exceeds the order. Uses the latest saved version of each invoice (Cancelled ignored). Only lines created from PO rows are matched; lines you add by hand are not. Quantities are matched in the PO line's own UOM – if you changed a line's UOM, it is flagged ⚠ and not counted.
