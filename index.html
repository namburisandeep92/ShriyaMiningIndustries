Our Story

Stone is our
Legacy

Shreya Mining is a fully integrated natural stone enterprise — from quarrying raw deposits in India's richest mineral belts to precision processing, manufacturing, and global export. We control every stage of the journey, ensuring unmatched consistency and quality.

Our stones grace the floors of European residences, the facades of landmark commercial buildings, and the terraces of luxury resorts worldwide. Each slab carries the character of the earth itself — ancient, enduring, and irreplaceable.

How We Work

The Complete
Stone Chain

01
Quarrying Unit
Our own quarries span prime mineral-rich zones. We use precision blasting and diamond wire cutting to extract blocks with minimal wastage and maximal quality.
→
02
Processing Unit
Advanced gang saws, calibration machines, and finishing lines transform raw blocks into precisely dimensioned slabs, tiles, cobbles, and custom formats.
→
03
Manufacturing
From cladding panels and wall tiles to pool copings and bespoke architectural pieces — we manufacture to exact international specifications and tolerances.
→
04
Export & Logistics
We handle end-to-end export documentation, container packing, phytosanitary compliance, and delivery to ports across Europe, the Middle East, and the Americas.
